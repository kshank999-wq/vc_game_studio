# The sample's takes

These six takes voice the lines of the sample game, *The Sunken Vault*. They
are placeholder performances, not recordings of actors: each was synthesized
with [Piper](https://github.com/rhasspy/piper) (MIT) using its LibriTTS
"high" voice — speaker 12 for Mara, speaker 7 for the explorer — then trimmed
and encoded as 48 kbps mono MP3.

The LibriTTS voice is trained on the LibriTTS corpus (Zen et al., 2019,
https://www.openslr.org/60/), licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Credit for the
voices goes to the LibriTTS corpus and its LibriVox readers.
